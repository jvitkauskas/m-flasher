M-Flasher for the web

Back up, restore and install firmware on an M-VAVE FM-1 or SMK-37, over USB MIDI, from a computer or a phone. Nothing leaves your device.

This browser can't reach MIDI devices

Use Chrome, Edge, Brave or Firefox on a computer or Android. On iOS, use an app with Web MIDI, such as MIDIWeb. Safari doesn't support it.

Connect the unit

Plug it in directly with a USB data cable, and switch it on. Close M-UPGRADE, other editors and music apps first.

This unit

    What to do

    Keep this tab in front, and don't unplug the unit.

    What this page can and can't do
    • On a unit running normal firmware: it starts a small helper program through the firmware's own update route. That writes a few KiB to the area firmware keeps for updates, then the unit runs the helper from RAM. When you finish, or after 5 minutes without this page asking anything, it goes back to its firmware.
    • With m-bootloader: hold PLAY (SEQ PLAY on the SMK-37) at power-on, choose MIDI MODE, and connect. That works with no firmware at all, and writes nothing until you ask.
    • Backups are the whole flash, byte for byte, in the same format as M-Flasher on a computer, and checked with a CRC the chip computes. Keep them safe.
    • Restoring covers firmware, settings and presets. The bootloader and key area are never written from here, nor the SMK-37's calibration and Bluetooth data.
    • You still need M-Flasher on a computer for a unit that won't start and has no m-bootloader, for download mode, and to put the stock bootloader back. Get M-Flasher.
